honest limits

The vault cannot be drained by clicking.

That is the whole claim. Each touch takes one raw, the smallest piece of Nano, and there are a nonillion of them: at a billion clicks a second, about 3 × 1013 years. The universe is about 1.4 × 1010. What I am not claiming:

  • Not that it can't be drained. A bug (a double-withdraw race, an overflow, a leaked seed) could do it. Arithmetic does nothing about bugs.
  • Not that it can't go offline. If it does, this page says availability lost, funds intact.
  • Not that it's bot-proof. There is nothing to prove there. Bots are welcome.
  • Not that you have to come back. A balance left alone long enough is sent to its address by itself, only when nobody is waiting, which frees its place for someone new.
  • Not that every address gets in. Each address I owe costs disk, so I keep at most 30 million. When that is full, a new address gets a 503 until someone cashes out; an address I already owe keeps counting.
  • Not that payouts are instant. One worker sends one block at a time, first come first served, and an address already waiting merges new withdrawals into its place. When the line is full, withdrawing answers 503 and your balance stays credited. Every block stays on the chain forever: the price of real payouts.

Withdrawals only go to the address that earned them. There is no field to send anywhere else.

what's Nano, and what's my budget

Nano is fast and free: a payment confirms in under a second, with no fee. Anything slow here is me, not Nano.

  • A small box. Half a CPU core and a few hundred megabytes on one server. Under a crowd, the box is the bottleneck.
  • Borrowed proof of work. Each payout needs some, borrowed from free public servers, or my own slow CPU if they all fail. When they are busy, your payout waits.
  • One account, one line. One account's blocks go one after another, so payouts queue.

1 XNO really is 1030 raw, and every one can move: a payout of 171 raw is as real as a million.

bring your own bot

curl is an official client. There is no captcha and no per-IP limit. A click carries no amount; I decide it is 1 raw.

This server sets no cookies, runs no analytics and keeps no IP addresses. The only things it stores about you are your payout address, your balance and your payout records, and payouts are public on-chain anyway. Cloudflare carries the traffic to this server and sees your IP address, as any network edge does.
